# EcoSense AI – 2-Minute Judge Demo Script

**Project**: EcoSense AI – Intelligent Building Energy Optimizer  
**Track**: Yuva Yodha Energy Tech Hackathon 2026 — Smart Buildings  
**Core Flow**: **SENSE → PREDICT → OPTIMIZE → RECOMMEND → MEASURE**  
**Prototype Nature**: Deterministic Physical/Behavioral Simulation & ML Prototype  

---

## Demo Script Timeline (120 Seconds)

### 0:00 – 0:20 | Introduction & The Problem
- **Speaker**: "Hello judges! Commercial buildings account for over 30% of commercial electricity consumption in India, yet significant energy is wasted cooling and lighting empty meeting rooms, or running full HVAC regardless of actual occupancy. This prototype evaluates a simulated commercial building to demonstrate how **EcoSense AI** transforms building operations into an occupant-aware, grid-responsive system."
- **Action**: Open the landing page (`http://localhost:3000`). Highlight the tagline *"Make Every Square Metre Smarter"* and click **"Launch Dashboard"**.

### 0:20 – 0:45 | Sense & Simulation Telemetry
- **Speaker**: "Here in the facility manager's cockpit, our architecture starts with **SENSE**. We monitor five simulated zones: Lobby, Office Floor, Meeting Room, Conference Room, and Cafeteria. All sensor streams are generated by our deterministic physics and behavioral simulation engine. Notice the top KPI cards showing simulated energy today, peak demand, occupancy, and an 89.6% temperature-based comfort compliance proxy."
- **Action**: Hover over the **KPI Cards** and point out the transparent **"SIMULATION RESULT"** and **"SIMULATED DATA"** badges.
- **Speaker**: "Notice the dual-axis Energy Analytics chart: this simulation estimates a 27.8% reduction compared with our defined fixed-schedule baseline."

### 0:45 – 1:05 | Predict & ML Demand Forecasting
- **Speaker**: "Next is **PREDICT**. The current prototype uses a Random Forest model trained on simulated time-series data. It uses historical power lags, calendar schedules, and ambient weather to forecast building electrical demand for the next 12 hours with an out-of-sample MAE of ~3.8 kW. The shaded area shows our approximate empirical prediction band (±1.645 × MAE)."
- **Action**: Scroll to the **AI Demand Forecast** section, showing simulated ground truth vs. predicted demand curves and model metrics ($R^2 \approx 0.94$).

### 1:05 – 1:25 | Optimize & AI Recommendations (Human-in-the-Loop)
- **Speaker**: "This leads to **OPTIMIZE & RECOMMEND**. Rather than an opaque black box, EcoSense AI delivers actionable recommendations with clear justifications, expected savings, and temperature comfort bounds. For example, notice this advisory: *Meeting Room is unoccupied during work hours—engage HVAC setback to save 4.8 kW*."
- **Action**: Click on a recommendation card. Review the popup details (diagnostic reason, expected kW savings, comfort impact, peak impact). Click **"Accept Recommendation"**. Notice the status badge instantly updates to **"ACCEPTED"**.

### 1:25 – 1:45 | Scenario Lab
- **Speaker**: "Judges, how do progressive levels of building automation compare? Let's open our **Scenario Lab**."
- **Action**: Click **"Scenario Lab"** in the navigation bar.
- **Speaker**: "Here we compare four operational strategies side-by-side against our fixed-schedule baseline:
  - **Scenario A**: Fixed Schedule Baseline (0% savings)
  - **Scenario B**: Occupancy-Aware Control (~11.5% simulated savings)
  - **Scenario C**: Occupancy + Demand Forecasting (~16.2% simulated savings)
  - **Scenario D**: Full EcoSense Optimization (**27.8% simulated energy reduction & 23.2% peak demand shaving**)."
- **Action**: Show the direct comparison bar charts and tabular breakdown of simulated energy, peak kW, operating cost, and comfort.

### 1:45 – 2:00 | Deployment Economics & Limitations
- **Speaker**: "Finally, in our **Deployment & Payback Calculator**, facility managers can test illustrative deployment estimates. For a simulated 5,000 sq.m facility, the illustrative simple payback is approximately **4 months** with CapEx of ₹200,000 and net annual savings of ₹597,000. All results are simulation-based evaluations running live on our local machine right now."
- **Action**: Navigate to **"Deployment & Economics"**, show the illustrative estimate disclaimer, and conclude presentation.
