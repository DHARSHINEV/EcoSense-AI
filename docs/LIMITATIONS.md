# EcoSense AI – Technical Limitations & Engineering Transparency

In accordance with strict hackathon scientific integrity standards, this prototype discloses all simplifying assumptions and technical limitations.

---

## 1. Prototype Limitations

1. **Simulated Telemetry vs. Physical Measurements**:
   - All time-series data, occupancy counts, temperature readings, and power consumption metrics shown in the prototype are **SIMULATED DATA** generated by a parameterized thermodynamic simulator.
   - Results have not yet been calibrated or validated against a physical building or hardware BMS testbed.

2. **Simplified Thermal Dynamics (RC Lumped-Capacitance Model)**:
   - The simulator assumes simplified single-node zone air capacitance and steady-state thermal conduction.
   - It does not perform full 3D Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) or transient EnergyPlus/DOE-2 building envelope simulations.

3. **Machine Learning Model Boundaries**:
   - The RandomForestRegressor is trained on simulated historical time-series data.
   - Model accuracy in real-world deployments will be directly dependent on real sensor signal-to-noise ratio, calibration drift, and seasonal distribution shifts.

4. **HVAC Control Interface Assumptions**:
   - The prototype assumes the building automation system (BMS) supports digital supervisory setpoint adjustments via standard protocols (BACnet IP, Modbus RTU, or MQTT).
   - In legacy pneumatic or manual standalone split AC installations, hardware retrofits (smart thermostats or IR blasters) are required before automated optimization can be realized.

5. **Grid Demand Response Dependency**:
   - Peak demand load-shifting and automated curtailment depend on local electric utility tariff structures (such as Time-of-Day tariffs) and formal demand response market rules, which vary across Indian state distribution companies (DISCOMs).

6. **Simplified Comfort Modeling**:
   - The comfort model uses an adaptive temperature band (22.0°C–26.0°C) with humidity penalties as a practical proxy.
   - It does not measure radiant temperature asymmetry, mean radiant temperature (MRT), local air velocity, or individual clothing/metabolic rates required for strict Fanger PMV/PPD compliance.

## 2. Anti-Hallucination & Provenance Declarations

- **No fabricated customer claims**: No partnerships, customer deployments, certifications, or awards are claimed.
- **Explicit labeling**: All system dashboards, API payloads, and analytical charts clearly differentiate:
  1. *Simulated Data* (ambient conditions, occupancy, zone telemetry)
  2. *Model Predictions* (near-term ML demand forecasts with error bars)
  3. *Optimization Recommendations* (actionable human-in-the-loop advisories)
  4. *Simulation Results* (comparative energy, peak kW, cost, and carbon savings)
